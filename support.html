Kippy Support

Kippy is a travel expense app for tracking and splitting costs on your trips. All of your data stays on your device.

Frequently asked

Where is my data stored?
Entirely on your device. Kippy has no accounts and no server. Deleting the app deletes your data, so export or settle up anything important first.

How does screenshot import work?
Kippy reads expense screenshots (like Apple Wallet transactions) using on-device text recognition. Images are processed locally and never leave your phone. Screenshot a transaction's detail view for best results.

I bought Full Access on a previous phone or reinstall. How do I get it back?
Open the upgrade screen and tap Restore Purchase. Your purchase is tied to your Apple ID.

Which languages does Kippy support?
English, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Spanish, French, Korean, and German. Kippy follows your device language and you can change it any time in Settings.
